Miranda Christ

I am a postdoc at MIT hosted by Shafi Goldwasser and Vinod Vaikuntanathan and a researcher at the Institute for Responsible Superintelligence (RESI). I recently completed my PhD in Computer Science at Columbia University, where I was a member of the Theory Group and the Crypto Lab. There, I was co-advised by Tal Malkin and Mihalis Yannakakis.

I am interested in practically motivated theoretical problems in cryptography. Recently I've been especially interested in the intersection of cryptography and machine learning, and have worked on watermarks for AI-generated content. You can read about some of my work in the Simons Institute's Polynomial Times magazine.

In addition to below, you can find my papers on my dblp and Google Scholar pages.

Email: mchrist at cs dot columbia dot edu
